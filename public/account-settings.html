Delete Account and Delete My Data

Use this page to submit authenticated deletion requests, manage consent, and track the purge status. Requests are soft-deleted first, then permanently purged after the restore window and retention period.

Default retention 30 days
Restore window 7 days
Support support@maxsas.ai

Delete My Data

This removes call sessions, transcripts, leads, campaign contacts, and related call artifacts while keeping billing records required for financial and legal purposes.

Before submitting, export anything you want to keep. Deleted data may not be recoverable after processing.

Delete Account

This schedules a full account deletion, immediately revokes access, and starts the restore window before the permanent purge job runs.

Account deletion is irreversible once the purge window expires. A support agent can restore the workspace only during the pending window.
Consent warnings
  • You will lose access to the workspace immediately after the request is accepted.
  • Billing, tax, and security records may be retained temporarily where legally required.
  • The request returns a tracking token. Keep it if you want to check status later.
