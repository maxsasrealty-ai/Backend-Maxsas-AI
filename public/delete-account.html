Account and data deletion information

Delete account and data

This page explains how to request deletion, what gets removed, what may be retained temporarily for legal, security, or billing reasons, and how to contact support. It is the official Google Play account deletion URL for Maxsas AI and Maxsas Realty AI.

How to request deletion

1. Sign in to the app or workspace associated with your account.

2. Open Settings and choose Delete Account or Delete My Data.

3. Review the warning, confirm the request, and save the tracking token shown after submission.

4. If you cannot sign in, contact support using the details below and include your workspace name, email address, and the request ID if you already have one.

What is deleted

  • Account access, user profile data, and tenant workspace access when you request full account deletion.
  • Call sessions, transcripts, transcripts segments, call events, lead extraction data, and related call artifacts when you request data deletion or account deletion.
  • Campaign contacts and outbound call requests that contain user-generated or lead-related personal data.

What may be retained temporarily

  • Billing, tax, invoice, reconciliation, and payment records required by law or for chargeback resolution.
  • Security and abuse-prevention logs, including audit records and deletion status tracking.
  • Temporary restoration metadata during the admin restore window before permanent purge.

Retention period

Full account deletion is soft-deleted immediately and then permanently purged after the restore window and retention period configured by the workspace. Default retention is 30 days and the default restore window is 7 days.

Restore window

An admin can restore a pending deletion before the purge deadline. Once permanent purge starts, the data cannot be recovered.
